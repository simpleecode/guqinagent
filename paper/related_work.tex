\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Guqin notation and performance-oriented symbolic music.}
Guqin notation digitization addresses complementary levels: \citet{li2026zeroshotjianzi} recognize the compositional structure of individual jianzi, whereas \citet{li2026jztab} extract structured content from full-page documents interleaving jianzipu and Chinese text. These works recover existing notation rather than select new performance actions.

Language-model-based transcription from jianzipu to modern notation includes duration prediction \cite{shi2024guqintranscription}. Our task is not a symmetric inverse: a melody underdetermines the performance actions needed to realize it.

Guitar tablature inference similarly selects among string--fret realizations of a pitch \cite{edwards2024miditotab}, while piano fingering considers hand placement and sequential execution difficulty \cite{iman2024pianofingering}. YuE2 uses editable melody-and-harmony scores to guide audio generation \cite{yuan2026yue2}. These settings motivate explicit planning, but guqin action selection additionally depends on inherited harmonic regions, hand positions, and compound techniques. Familiarity with technique terminology alone may not support context-sensitive use, motivating guqin-specific training rather than assuming such competence in general-purpose models.

\paragraph{Tool-interactive planning and revision.}
ReAct interleaves reasoning and environment actions, while Toolformer and ToolLLM investigate tool-use learning and data construction \cite{yao2023react,schick2023toolformer,qin2024toolllm}. Self-Refine and Reflexion use iterative feedback to improve model behavior; CRITIC specifically studies revision driven by external tool outputs \cite{madaan2023selfrefine,shinn2023reflexion,gou2024critic}. \system uses a shared executable notation state to check pitch realizations and issue conservative, event-level warnings. These checks inform subsequent edits but do not determine musical suitability on their own.

\paragraph{Learning from interaction trajectories.}
AgentTuning and FireAct demonstrate the value of supervised interaction trajectories for language agents \cite{zeng2024agenttuning,chen2023fireact}. More closely related, RetroGen reconstructs process supervision from final expert artifacts for evidence-grounded generation \cite{huang2026retrogen}. Our expert-guided inverse trajectories similarly start from a target artifact, but require the teacher to execute selections, checks, and revisions in the guqin runtime; only public actions and observations enter student training. GEPA instead optimizes prompts by reflecting on execution feedback without updating model weights, providing a distinct comparison to trajectory fine-tuning \cite{agrawal2026gepa}.
